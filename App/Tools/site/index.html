How it works

One request, four steps. Here's one playing out:

  1. 1

    Say it

    Type or speak what you want, in your own words. Your own AI reads it: ChatGPT, Claude, or a model on your Mac.

  2. 2

    It looks things up

    Balances on every network, prices and charts, routes through Jupiter, Relay and Uniswap. A second reading checks it understood you.

  3. 3

    It lays out the route

    Each step is a card: what it does, with which protocol, and your money now → after.

  4. 4

    You confirm, it runs

    Press Confirm and touch Touch ID. Each step lights up as it lands, and the last card shows what you have.

Why it's safe

Your keys never leave your Mac, the AI never signs, and nothing moves without your finger.

Keys sealed in your Mac

The wallet is made on your Mac and sealed by its Secure Enclave. It never leaves the device: no iCloud backup, nothing kept on a server.

Touch ID for every move

Each move asks for your fingerprint, and one touch covers one move. Or confirm on a Ledger and keep the bulk there.

The AI can't press Confirm

The model reads, looks things up and proposes; it never holds a key. Only the Confirm button on a card signs. A typed "yes", or a web page trying to talk it into one, moves nothing.

Every request read twice

Each command and plan gets a second reading. If the two disagree, nothing runs: the move waits as a card for you to check.

A signer that says no

Signing happens in a separate process with no network access. It refuses unlimited approvals, permits and blind signatures, and caps what a call to an unknown contract can risk.

Checked before it's sent

A new address asks Touch ID with the whole address on screen. Contracts are checked for age, verified code and scam reports; lookalike sites are blocked.

Signed and notarized by Apple. Updates are signed too and install themselves.

Questions

Who holds the keys?
You do. The wallet is made on your Mac and sealed in its Secure Enclave; you confirm moves with Touch ID, or with a Ledger. Nothing is kept on a server.
Can the AI move my money by itself?
No. It can look things up and lay out a move; only your Confirm, with Touch ID, carries one out.
Which networks does it work with?
Solana, Hyperliquid, Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Polygon, Robinhood Chain, NEAR and Tempo, through Jupiter, Kamino, Aave, Morpho, Relay, Uniswap and NEAR Intents.
Which AI does it use?
Yours: a ChatGPT Plus or Pro subscription, a Claude subscription through Claude Code, OpenRouter, an API key, or a model running on your Mac.
What does it cost?
The app is a free download. You pay the networks' fees and your own AI plan.
What do I need?
A Mac with macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Updates install themselves.
